nailfile - privacy policy

last updated: april 20, 2026

we built nailfile so that almost everything stays on your phone. this page explains what does and doesn't leave your device, and why.

what we collect

  • photos you add to your diary or use for try-on. these are stored on your device, not in the cloud.
  • a random anonymous id we use to count your free try-ons and link your subscription. it isn't tied to your name, email, or apple id.
  • basic usage events (which screen you opened, whether a try-on succeeded). no personal content.
  • subscription status and purchase history, handled by revenuecat.

we do not collect your name, email, address, contacts, location, or anything you type into your diary notes.

what happens to your photos

diary photos stay on your device. nothing is uploaded.

try-on photos are sent to our secure server only at the moment you tap generate. the server forwards them to google's gemini model to render the new look, then sends the result back to you. we delete try-on uploads from our server within 30 days. we don't use your photos to train any model.

third parties we use

  • google gemini — generates try-on images. processed temporarily, not retained by them for training under our terms.
  • cloudflare — hosts the server that brokers try-on requests and stores temporary images.
  • revenuecat — manages your subscription and credits.
  • telemetrydeck — anonymous, privacy-respecting analytics. no personal data, no advertising ids.

your choices

  • delete a diary entry to remove its photos from your device.
  • uninstall the app to remove everything stored locally.
  • email us to request deletion of any try-on images still on our server, or to ask what we have on you.

children

nailfile is not directed at anyone under 13.

changes

if we change anything material we'll update the date at the top and call it out in-app.
